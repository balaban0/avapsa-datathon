# Hacettepe Datathon 2025 - Customer Segmentation and Data Analytics

This repository contains the data science project we developed as **Team AVAPSA** for **Datathon 2025**, organized by the **Hacettepe University Artificial Intelligence Community**.

We completed the project successfully, placing **11th out of 80 teams**.

The project was developed on **Kaggle** because of its fast data processing and collaboration advantages.

## Project Goal and Scope

The project aims to use a company's raw customer data to generate insights that guide its marketing and operational strategies. Our main focus areas were:

- **Customer Segmentation:** Grouping customers by their shopping habits (Loyal, Potential, At-Risk, etc.).
- **RFM Analysis:** Valuing customers with the Recency, Frequency, and Monetary metrics.
- **Demographic and Financial Analysis:** Analyzing the effect of education level, age, and income on spending.
- **Strategic Recommendations:** Action plans to reduce churn (customer loss) risk and improve company efficiency.

## Tools and Methodology

- **Data Analysis:** `Pandas`, `NumPy`
- **Visualization:** `Seaborn`, `Matplotlib` (correlation matrices, distribution plots, and pie charts)
- **Statistical Approach:** Outlier analysis, feature engineering, and data standardization.

## Key Findings

- **Segmentation:** We found that a large share of the customer base falls into the "Regular Customer" category, and that targeted campaigns are needed to increase the proportion of "Loyal Customers".
- **Correlation:** We identified positive correlations between income level and spending on certain product categories, and proposed targeted advertising strategies based on them.
